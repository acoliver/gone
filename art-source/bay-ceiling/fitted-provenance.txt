# Source-fitted ceiling panels

The five fitted exports derive their broad outer and inner rim contours from `hunyuan-panel-seed-4208.glb`, generated by Hunyuan3D-2mv with seed 4208. The generated GLB, approved references, source guide and generated colour swatches remain unchanged. `assets/props/bay-ceiling/fitted-source.blend` retains the imported source in a hidden collection, alongside the editable fitted variants and separate mounting heads. Source and guide SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `assets/props/bay-ceiling/manifest.json` and independently checked in `fitted-measurements.json`.

## Fitting and authored profile adjustment

The measured source contours at raw Y=0.015 m are aligned, faired and uniformly width-scaled. A smooth central-span map fits length while retaining end-contour scale. The intact fitted footprint is 1.140 by 0.560 m, at object and export scale one. The 28 elongated through-slots retain their 4-by-7 arrangement, 108 by 22 mm nominal dimensions, 24.552 mm minimum inter-slot ligament and 28.459 mm minimum slot-to-inner-rim clearance.

The source height measurement gives a 3.154 mm face drop below its rim. The fitted shape deliberately increases that drop to 18 mm for the isolated shape target. This authored adjustment is recorded separately from the source measurements. Five rings use the same outer/inner guide rays, at fractions [0, 0.23, 0.44, 0.84, 1] and top heights [0, 0, 0, -0.017, -0.018] metres. They create a flat mounting flange and a two-slope pressed return to the perforated face. No primitive rectangle replaces either measured contour. The closed shell uses paired vertices with a 2 mm vertical separation, including its slot and outline returns.

The authoring frame is Blender XY plan with +Z up and front -Y. `export_yup=True` produces glTF XZ plan with +Y up and front +Z. Independent checks read the exported GLBs, not Blender bounds.

## Variants and actual mounting interfaces

Each GLB contains one connected closed perforated panel and four separately tagged closed flanged-head solids. Each head is 12 mm in diameter, 4.4 mm high and 240 triangles. These are captive-head representations without threaded shanks or extra bores. They sit on the top flange at local Y=0 in glTF; the corresponding underside support plane is local Y=-0.002. The manifest provides both sets of actual attachment points per variant, their normal, hardware object names and body counts. It does not imply that scene supports have already moved.

The flat flange remains fixed in the warp and sag variants. Independent rays around every head footprint verify complete flat seating. The torn upper-right corner removes its former mounting region, so its fourth anchor moves to glTF [0.266521, 0, -0.271601] m. The other three anchors stay fixed. Runtime placement and material binding belong to the parent integration stage.

## UVs and image-derived bake

Top and bottom UV0 use overlapping normalized planar coordinates, u=x/1.14+0.5 and v=y/0.56+0.5 in Blender. Each outline or slot return edge has a separate projected strip: its top UV follows the edge, and its bottom UV shifts 2 mm into the metal-side colour bank. The strips have nonzero area and sample the same geometry-masked edge paint as the broad face. The steep rim retains planar projection and therefore stretches the texture in those bands. Hardware uses local dominant-plane face projection.

`tools/prepare_bay_ceiling_material.py` rebakes all five 2048-by-1024 albedos from `generated-charcoal-paint-swatch.png`, with `generated-aged-steel-swatch.png` confined to oxide masks. Existing image-derived colour calibration is retained. Each damaged outline now drives its own oxide, lip, dust, normal and roughness maps. The bake asserts all variant contours against the geometry manifest and records the manifest/source hashes. Existing `roughness.png` and `normal.png` remain intact maps for the current material helper. Matching per-variant normal/roughness maps are available for the parent to bind. No runtime material or global shader code is changed here.

## Verification and evidence

`tools/blender/test_bay_ceiling_geometry.py` tests source residuals, contour fit, ligament clearance, profile depth, contiguous material removal, bent lips and fixed/relocated mounts. `tools/test_prepare_bay_ceiling_material.py` tests source colour transfer and variant-specific coverage/masks. Tests were added before the corresponding implementation.

`tools/blender/verify_bay_ceiling.py` independently verifies all five GLBs: position-welded topology across UV/normal seams; one panel body with Euler characteristic -54; four head bodies with Euler characteristic 2 each; watertightness, positive volume, consistent winding, nondegenerate triangles, scale one, dimensions, 2 mm paired thickness, UV area, opaque neutral material, attachment coordinates and installed clearance. Per variant, 140 rays check clear slot interiors, 24 check solid ligaments and 64 check flat head seats. Another 54 and 57 rays check removed regions for torncorner and brokenedge. The results are in `fitted-measurements.json` and `tmp/verify-stasis-ceiling/geometry/export-metrics.json`.

The neutral render set is written to `tmp/verify-stasis-ceiling/geometry/`: studio, plan and edge views for all five variants, plus torncorner-detail and brokenedge-detail. `renders.json` lists exact paths; `render-settings.json` records CPU Cycles, 48 samples, 2000-by-1400 output and the geometry manifest hash. These images are evidence for the parent's image-comparer, without visual acceptance claimed here.

## Limits

Sheet thickness is a 2 mm vertical extrusion, not a constant normal offset on steep or bent surfaces. The verifier does not perform exhaustive 3D triangle-intersection certification. The slot/outline return UV projection intentionally overlaps the adjacent metal colour bank; the sloped rim remains stretched. Mounting heads omit hidden shanks, threads and bores. GLBs retain a neutral material for shape review; the rebaked paint and matching damaged relief maps still require parent binding. Scene carriers, placement, lighting and presenter code were not edited.
