# Selection notes

`terminal-nobay-finished.glb` is the selected body from `termmodel4`. It provides the clean, open display recess and a flat rear wall fit. The working display uses the exported `ScreenSocket` rather than a guessed offset. The matching tray and keyboard come from `assembly3/assembly.blend`; the exported selection includes the olive tray, returns, mounting flange, gasket, gussets, bolts, keyboard housing, and keys. The terminal body and keyboard-tray files share the Blender terminal-space origin.

The source renders and candidate images are kept beside this record. `build.py` and `finish_terminal.py` document the body generation and final fit. `build_keyboard.py` and `assemble.py` document keyboard and tray construction.
