# Terminal prop sources

The terminal shell and keyboard tray were generated from FLUX.2-klein-4B product images, then reconstructed and fitted with the local Hunyuan3D-2mv MLX port. The shell build and finishing scripts are retained here, as are the keyboard reconstruction and terminal-space assembly scripts. `assets/props/terminal/terminal.glb` is the selected shell at 32,506 triangles with four embedded images. `keyboard-tray.glb` is the tray and keyboard exported together from the fitted Blender assembly at 43,112 triangles with 29 embedded images, so both retain the same terminal-local coordinates.

The selected visual references are the concept reference and the `s412` and `s413` front and three-quarter FLUX outputs. The final fit preserves the open screen recess, a 0.54 × 0.28 m screen plane, and the wall-back/floor-zero placement. The broken station treatment is runtime material and pose variation; it does not duplicate geometry.
