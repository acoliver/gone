Part B: full non-turbo Hunyuan3D-2mv, float16, 30 steps, CFG 5, octree 384, chunks 50000, mc_level 0, box_v 1.01, no component pruning, seeds 410/411/412 per object. These settings match art-source/hatch/hunyuan/provenance.json. Front and left references are generated by FLUX edits as in hatch generation/run.py, then white-component alpha-matted. All intermediate views and masks are retained. HF_HOME=/Volumes/XS1000/hf-cache; HF_HUB_OFFLINE=1; bytecode disabled; NUMBA_CACHE_DIR, MPLCONFIGDIR, TMPDIR, XDG_CACHE_HOME point inside this output directory. Metal errors/hangs wait 60 seconds and retry once.

Driver picks are read from picks.json after REFINE_READY; absent picks time out after 1200 seconds, polled every 60 seconds, then first successful seed per object is used. Rendering has no visual acceptance or ranking.
