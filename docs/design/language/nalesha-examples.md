# Nalesha examples and story synopsis

> **Status:** English companion to the v0.13 Nalesha language material, dated 22 October 2026. The synopsis calls the project Naleya and uses the same script and grammar. The final language name remains open. The Spanish story bible, [`docs/design/biblia.md`](../biblia.md), takes precedence on story facts. Native glyphs appear in all three archived source PDFs; text extraction does not preserve the artwork.

These translation examples illustrate grammar and do not establish plot facts. The synopsis passages are story material and include spoilers. Literal English preserves word order and grammatical details; natural English supplies idiomatic phrasing and known character genders.

## Synopsis notes

The awakening and present danger use bare ongoing predicates. Revealed backstory takes **-pa**. **-pu** marks the expected future; a time phrase or condition anchors events beyond the immediate next moment. An order quoted from the past is spoken in its own present. A character's belief does not establish that the belief is true.

Numbers remain base eight. The elapsed-time phrase is **1750 octal = 1000 Earth decimal**. The expected remaining voyage is **36 octal = 30 Earth decimal**. Octal **1750** is formed by one 512-place (**nata**), seven 64s (**naka**), five 8s (**nala**), and zero units; zero is omitted in the spoken place phrase. **Tewa ki tanu** explicitly means Earth years, so no duration is assigned to a native year. Multiple **naki** is rendered approximately as billions.

The synopsis reports the captain's decision and the records' account of Earth's devastation. It does not prove that every human died, confirm arrival, or decide whether the crew's people will offer refuge.
