The exact same sofa, same camera, same grey-green steel frame, same layout, but after one thousand years in a sealed, dry, sterile starship cabin with no mould, no water and no plants. The foam inside all six cushions has oxidised and crumbled to yellow-brown powder, so the charcoal-grey woven upholstery covers have collapsed: the three seat cushions are sunken, slack and deeply wrinkled, lowest in the middle, the three back cushions have slumped down into baggy bottom-heavy folds against the frame. The charcoal fabric has faded unevenly to a dusty lighter grey with a slight warm brown cast, and is brittle; it has split open in a few ragged frayed tears along seams and folds, showing crumbly yellow-brown foam crust inside. Small yellow-brown foam crumbs and powder have spilled from the tears onto the seats and the floor in front. The oatmeal wool throw has faded, split along the arm crease and hangs down the side of the arm almost to the floor. The steel frame has dull faded paint with some small rust spots at the edges. Fine grey-brown dust everywhere. Plain neutral studio lighting, plain light grey background.
