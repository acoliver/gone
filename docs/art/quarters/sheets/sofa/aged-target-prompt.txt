The exact same sofa, same camera, same steel frame, same layout, but after one thousand years in a sealed, dry, sterile starship cabin with no mould, no water and no plants. The foam inside all six cushions has oxidised and crumbled to brown powder, so the faded oatmeal upholstery covers have collapsed: the three seat cushions are sunken, slack and deeply wrinkled, lowest in the middle, the three back cushions have slumped down into baggy bottom-heavy folds against the frame. The brittle fabric has split open in a few ragged frayed tears along seams and folds, showing crumbly tan-brown foam crust inside. Small brown foam crumbs and powder have spilled from the tears onto the seats and the floor in front. The throw blanket has split along the arm crease and hangs down the side of the arm almost to the floor, faded and brittle. Fine grey-brown dust everywhere. Plain neutral studio lighting, plain background.
