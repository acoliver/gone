# Smoke symptom presyncope trial

This is a reversible presentation trial. The game does not cause unconsciousness, death, a blackout, loss of movement, or loss of aim. Escaping remains available. The design bible leaves death and retry presentation undecided.

The simulation advances once after `game.tick()` on each root physics tick. It samples the accepted player eye position, so a movement crossing is reflected on the next root tick. Exposure is full in the bay for x <= 5.5, y in 0..3.2, and |z| <= 4. It tapers linearly to zero at x=6, the hallway boundary. Dose rises by exposure/(18*60) each tick. Zero exposure recovers at 1/(8*60) per tick; partial exposure blends the rise and recovery. Dose is clamped to 0..1. No exposure or events accrue until `phase.look_allowed()`, including the AwakeInPod state immediately after the existing 282-tick wake.

The pure `sim/smoke_symptoms.gd` owns dose and deterministic event IDs. The simulation reserves each full WAV duration plus 12 logical silent ticks before scheduling another event. Production `app/main.gd` also holds an in-flight audio-completion latch and waits at least 200000 microseconds after `AudioStreamPlayer.finished` before starting the next queued event. The latch and elapsed-time check protect against batched physics frames. Queued events are discarded when dose reaches zero. A production windowed test injected a finished event and observed 200589 microseconds before playback. Direct low-FPS mixed-audio PCM silence was not measured, and listener approval remains pending. Cues use one non-positional player. The first breath is delayed about 0.35 seconds. The cough stem has a quiet lead of 0.57 seconds, so its active burst follows its scheduled file start. Cough cadence targets about 7 seconds at low dose and 3.5 seconds at maximum. Breath cadence targets 2.4 to 1.2 seconds. Cough has priority; breaths must leave 0.2 seconds of silence and are skipped or deferred when they conflict. High-dose symptoms cap without changing control. The cue pulses are render-only samples of simulation state.

The fullscreen shader targets the periphery using aspect-corrected screen coordinates and `smoothstep(0.52, 0.95, radius)`. Everything at radius <= 0.52 remains unchanged, including at peak cough and breath pulses. Dose still drives gradual desaturation with a maximum 0.72 blend toward luminance, a two-pixel ghost offset, and four-pixel peripheral blur. Sustained attenuation is capped at 12%, reduced from 28%. A persistent neutral veil adds `0.035 * peripheral * clamp(dose, 0, 1)` to each RGB channel after attenuation. Its addition stays below 0.04, vanishes in the protected center and at zero dose, and fades monotonically during dose recovery even when the source is black. At maximum outer-periphery dose with no pulse, black becomes (0.035, 0.035, 0.035) and white becomes (0.915, 0.915, 0.915).

The cough and breath haze color and blend strengths are unchanged: (0.32, 0.38, 0.42), weighted by `peripheral * (cough_pulse * 0.26 + breath_pulse * 0.14)`. The existing smooth attack and decay envelopes remain unchanged. Pulses can outlast the instant dose reaches zero; the persistent veil cannot. The effect changes neither input nor camera/aim and adds no screen flash. Runtime viewport-routed mouse motion at wake, zero-dose, full-dose, and recovered stages passed 15 checks. Controlled captures at 2304x1296 confirmed the numeric center exactly. Preliminary image QA on earlier tuning found an overly dark mid-frame at 28% attenuation and indistinct hall recovery. The final 12%-attenuation/3.5%-veil tuning has numeric compiled-shader checks, but no further visual approval; there is no claim of a final visual pass. Human playtesting must establish whether the faint veil is noticeable in the unlit hall without making it appear lit, whether bay dimness remains natural, and whether pulse contrast and comfort are acceptable.

Numerical verification on Godot 4.7.2 passed the guarded suite (309 tests, zero failures) and a hidden Metal/Forward+ compiled-shader probe (22 source/pulse/dose cases, 158 assertions, zero failures). The probe wrote numeric samples only, with no PNGs or visual judgments. Protected-center pixels were bit-exact. Maximum channel error against the equations was 0.001868, within one 8-bit step. Black-source outer-periphery recovery at doses 1, 0.75, 0.5, 0.25, and 0 produced grayscale values 9/255, 7/255, 4/255, 2/255, and 0 respectively. Logs and numeric samples are under `tmp/smoke-final-tuning-20261003/`.

## Audio derivatives

All three smoke stems are provisional listening material derived from local research under `tmp/opening-audio/smoke-progression/`. Their CC0 source preview is LindaBarrans #800927, official HQ MP3 preview SHA-256 `5f6c115dcb10550c0da1846cc7e6cb1d5093e9257137691f0b1a33836bda915c`; the cached Freesound page identifies CC0 1.0. PCM24 exports do not restore the lossy preview detail.

- `wake-breath-mild-reference.wav`: exact composite breath [2.55, 3.30) seconds, source cut [42.30498866, 43.05498866), SHA-256 `2be42af1efd054f3be18b0f29320b1c586ad28e0a3e3540365d0218e7016dc41`.
- `linda-pulse-b-provisional.wav`: source cut [47.45, 48.40), SHA-256 `4e4d465aa8209f72abf01e5067fece12c44bd4519d5156e5ad510aa05ea9fbe4`. It is used only as an unapproved higher-intensity listening trial. The uncalibrated classifier gives Gasp 0.543, Speech 0.178, and Snort 0.454. It barely differs from the baseline in RMS. Those scores neither prove effort nor rule out speech; no claim that it definitively sounds labored or contains no words is made. Pulse A is excluded because Snort outranks Gasp and the recorded tail is truncated.
- `cough-mid-isolated.wav`: exact composite cut [3.43, 4.44), corresponding to #835646 source [11.22, 12.23), SHA-256 `144818d7e0de9685e8026dcbc1228bdd88d756f4ec102ece21f9904166eff65a`. The active burst occupies about [0.57, 0.94) seconds of the stem. No #444723 material is used.

The source previews and sample intervals are documented in the ignored research report. The wake WAV and its importer settings are unchanged. Smoke assets import as uncompressed PCM16 (`compress/mode=0`), without normalization, trim, or loop. Breath pulse runtime gain is capped at +5 dB; cough gain is +1 dB maximum. The measured exported peaks are -15.73 dBTP for pulse B and -7.21 dBTP for cough. With maximum runtime gains these become approximately -10.73 and -6.21 dBTP. These are numerical checks, not listening approval.
